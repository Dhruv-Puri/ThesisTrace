Certified Robustness for Image Classifiers

Abstract
We propose a smoothing method that gives certified robustness for image classifiers.

1. Introduction
The problem of adversarial examples limits real-world use.

2. Method
We introduce a randomized smoothing algorithm.

3. Experiments
We evaluate on large-scale ImageNet and other real-world datasets. Our method outperforms the baseline and results show certified accuracy of 61%.

4. Conclusion
The method can be deployed in production pipelines.
