Efficient Graph Sampling

Abstract
We propose a graph sampling method and evaluate it on citation graphs.

1. Introduction
Smith et al. deployed a similar sampler in production at a large retailer [12]. Prior systems have been used in production for millions of users.

2. Related Work
Existing systems were deployed at several hospitals and banks.

3. Method
We design a neighbourhood sampler.

4. Experiments
We evaluate on Cora and PubMed. Our sampler outperforms the baseline sampler.
