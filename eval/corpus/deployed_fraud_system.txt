Fraud Scoring at Scale

Abstract
We present a fraud model.

1. Method
We develop a gradient-boosted pipeline.

2. Experiments
We evaluate on a benchmark. Existing systems do not scale, and our model outperforms the baseline, which will be shown in Table 2.

3. Results
We ran a pilot deployment with a partner bank. The model was deployed in production at the bank and is used in production for 3M transactions a day.
