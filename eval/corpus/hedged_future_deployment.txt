Compact Keyword Spotting

Abstract
We propose a compact keyword spotting network and evaluate it on a public benchmark.

1. Method
We design a depthwise-separable architecture.

2. Experiments
We evaluate on Speech Commands. Our model outperforms the baseline by 3 points.

3. Conclusion
We plan to deploy the model in production next year. It could be used in real-world deployment on smart speakers. A field trial may follow.
