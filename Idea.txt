ThesisTracer - AI Research & Technology Intelligence Platform
One-line idea
Upload a research paper, patent, technical report, or product document → Paper2Reality explains what it does, extracts the evidence, checks its claims, evaluates reproducibility and technology readiness, and tells you what needs to happen to turn the research into a real-world product.

The important shift is that we're not building a paper summarizer.
We're building a research-to-commercialization intelligence tool.

🔍 3. ClaimCheck — Evidence Verification
This could become one of the strongest features.
The system extracts major claims from the paper.
For example:
"Our algorithm reduces energy consumption by 50%."

Then:
CLAIM
 ↓
Find supporting passages
 ↓
Semantic similarity
 ↓
NLI / entailment model
 ↓
Evidence assessment

Result
🟢 Supported
Evidence found on Page 7: energy consumption decreased from 100 kWh to 50 kWh.

Or:
🔴 Insufficient Evidence
The paper makes the claim, but no experiment directly supporting the 50% reduction was identified.

Or:
🟡 Partially Supported
The paper reports a 47–52% reduction under laboratory conditions, but does not demonstrate the claimed reduction in real-world conditions.

Important: don't call this "fact checking" in the sense of determining whether a scientific claim is objectively true. You're checking whether the paper itself provides sufficient evidence for the claim.
That's much more defensible.
🧪 4. Reproducibility Score
Now we go beyond NLP.
The system asks:
Can someone else actually reproduce this research?

It evaluates:
Reproducibility checklist
Component	Status
Dataset available	✅
Source code available	✅
Model details	✅
Hyperparameters	⚠️
Preprocessing	⚠️
Random seed	❌
Environment/dependencies	❌
Experimental procedure	✅


Then:
Reproducibility: 72/100
For computational papers, the advanced agent can attempt to:
Find GitHub
     ↓
Find dataset
     ↓
Create environment
     ↓
Run code
     ↓
Reproduce experiment
     ↓
Compare results

Example:
Published accuracy: 94.2%
Reproduced accuracy: 92.8%

Then it investigates possible causes.
🚀 5. Technology Readiness — The BIG differentiator
This is where the project becomes commercially interesting.

"Does the research work?"

we ask:
"How close is this technology to being useful in the real world?"

Use the established TRL 1–9 framework.
Example
Technology Readiness: TRL 5 / 9
Validated in a relevant environment
Evidence

🟢 Laboratory validation
🟢 Initial testing
🟡 Limited real-world testing
🔴 No large-scale deployment
And critically:
Why?
The AI cites the actual evidence from the document.

Paper → Stats + Summary + NER + Key Concepts + TF-IDF
Phase 2 — Evidence
Claims → Evidence retrieval → NLI
Phase 3 — Research Intelligence
Methodology + datasets + models + limitations + structured experiment
Phase 4 — Reproducibility
Code discovery → environment → experiment → result comparison
Phase 5 — Technology Readiness
Evidence → TRL 1–9 → justification
Phase 6 — Commercialization
TRL → risks → missing validation → recommended next steps → executive report
That way, you have a fully usable NLP project very early, but the architecture has a path toward something much more ambitious.
And the most important thing: don't let the LLM make up the TRL score or evidence. Every important assessment should be tied to specific passages, experiments, results, or external evidence. That's what can turn this from a flashy AI demo into a genuinely useful research tool.
